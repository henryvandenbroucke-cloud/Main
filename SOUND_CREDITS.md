# Sound credits

Every sound in Blockhollow is a real recording under an open licence. The files were trimmed, turned to mono, evened out in loudness and re-encoded as small MP3s by `tools/build_sounds.py`, which packs them into `src/sounds.js`. Under CC BY-SA, those edited versions are shared under the same licence as their originals. Only the cricket chirps, frog croaks, bee buzz and wind are still made by the game itself.
