# The GTA 6 Leak That Came From a Hotel TV

**Virality score: 91/100.**
- **Timing:** GTA 6 launches on **19 November 2026**, about a week after this was written. Launch week is the moment of peak search interest.
- **Demand is proven:** existing Shorts on this story have 24M ("How They Caught the GTA 6 Hacker"), 11M ("The Teen Who Leaked GTA 6 From A Hotel Room") and 10M views.
- **Competition is high, but ours is different:** almost all of those are commentary or reaction clips. Ours is a fully animated heist story in the Brink's-short style, with a callback ending that ties into launch week.

## Respectful handling
- The person is **not named** and **not caricatured**. He was a teenager, and the court considered his mental health. The story is about the hack, not about mocking him.
- **No Rockstar or GTA logos and no game art** are used. The studio is shown as a generic office, and "GTA 6" appears only as plain caption text.

## Facts and sources
- **The leak:** on 18 Sept 2022 a forum user posted about **90 videos (around 50 minutes)** from a test build of GTA 6. Rockstar confirmed the footage was real. Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2022/09/18/grand-theft-auto-vi-leak-and-hacker-rockstar), [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/rockstar-suffers-major-gta-6-leak-heres-what-we-know).
- **The hotel:** the hacker was on bail for earlier hacks (Nvidia, BT/EE) and his laptop had been confiscated. Police had moved him to a Travelodge for his own protection after rivals published his details online.
  - He used the hotel TV, an Amazon Fire TV Stick and a newly bought phone to get into Rockstar's internal Slack.
  - He posted demands there and threatened to release the source code.
  - City of London Police found him in the hotel room.
  - Sources: [Gfinity (trial reporting)](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/gta-6-leaker-travelodge-tv-fire-stick), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gta-6-hacker-is-sentenced-to-an-indefinite-hospitalization-194251395.html).
- **The sentence (December 2023):** Southwark Crown Court gave him an **indefinite hospital order**. The court said he had told clinicians he intended to return to cybercrime as soon as possible. Source: [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gta-6-hacker-who-intended-to-return-to-cyber-crime-as-soon-as-possible-sentenced-to-an-indefinite-hospital-order?mid=1).
- **The trailer:** Trailer 1 (December 2023) got **90,421,491 views in 24 hours**. Guinness World Records recognised it as the most-viewed video game reveal on YouTube in 24 hours. Source: [Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/news/2023/12/gta-6-trailer-pulls-90-million-views-in-24-hours-sets-new-world-records).
- **Release:** 19 November 2026. Source: [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/gta-6-release-details-1236846230/).
