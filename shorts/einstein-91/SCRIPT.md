# Einstein's 9 × 10 = 91 (the lesson that never happened)

**Virality score: 90/100.**
- **Demand is proven:** the two biggest Shorts telling this story have about 26M ("Einstein's Math Lesson...") and 15M ("The Day Einstein Made a Mistake 🧠") views.
- **Every top video tells it as true.** Our angle is unique: we tell the famous story first (viewers recognise it and stay), then flip it. It never happened, and the ending turns the story's own moral back on the audience. The closest myth-bust Short ("Einstein never said E=mc2") has 1.3M, so debunks of Einstein myths do travel.

## The hook
- **Frame 1:** an extreme close-up of chalk finishing the "1" in "9 × 10 = 91", with a single chalk scrape.
- The camera snaps back to a classroom of students bursting out laughing, while Einstein (wild white hair) stands frozen with the chalk.
- **The first spoken line is the whole famous story:** "Einstein once wrote nine times ten equals ninety-one."

## Facts and sources
- **The viral story:** Einstein writes the nine times table on the board, gets 9 × 10 = 91, the students laugh, and he says he got nine right and nobody congratulated him.
  - AAP FactCheck rated it **false** on 27 June 2022.
  - The **Albert Einstein Archives** (Hebrew University of Jerusalem) said: "We... have never heard of this story/quote and therefore it is most probably not correct."
  - The Einstein Papers Project called it "without doubt a fake quote".
  - Other circulating versions name only an unnamed maths teacher. Source: [AAP FactCheck](https://www.aap.com.au/factcheck/story-about-einsteins-maths-mistake-doesnt-add-up/).
- **The archives:** they hold tens of thousands of Einstein documents.
- **"Einstein failed maths" is a myth too:**
  - In 1935 a rabbi in Princeton showed him a newspaper column headlined "Greatest living mathematician failed in mathematics".
  - Einstein laughed: "I never failed in mathematics. Before I was fifteen I had mastered differential and integral calculus."
  - Sources: [TIME, "Did Einstein flunk math?"](https://content.time.com/time/specials/packages/article/0,28804,1936731_1936743_1936758,00.html), Walter Isaacson's *Einstein: His Life and Universe* (2007), and [Britannica](https://WWW.Britannica.com/one-good-fact/did-einstein-ever-fail-math).
- **View counts** are from a YouTube Shorts search on 11 Nov 2026.
