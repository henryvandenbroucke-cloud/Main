## Facts (sourced)
- **The locked caddy:**
  - Tea was initially an expensive luxury, which is why many tea canisters have a lock.
  - It was prepared by the lady of the house in the drawing room, in front of family and guests.
  - She commonly kept the caddy key on her chatelaine, so servants couldn't help themselves.
  - Sources: [V&A tea canister record](https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O78523/tea-canister-courtauld-louisa), [Tea caddy (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_caddy).
- **The tax:** Import duty on tea reached about 119%, which fuelled smuggling and adulteration.
  - Source: [Commutation Act 1784 (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutation_Act_1784).
- **Smuggling:** By 1784, smuggled tea is estimated to have made up about two-thirds of all tea drunk in Britain.
  - Source: [Mary Twining (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Twining).
- **Smouch:** 18th-century slang for fake tea made from hedge leaves (ash for black tea) that were dried, curled and dyed. Spent leaves were also dried and resold as fresh.
  - Sources: [Tea fraud and adulteration](https://teas.co.uk/wiki/tea-fraud-and-adulteration/), [Destination Tea](https://destinationtea.com/tea-vocabulary-teapoys-and-smouch/).
- **The end:** The Commutation Act of 1784, passed by Prime Minister William Pitt the Younger, cut the duty from about 119% to 12.5%, and the smuggling trade largely collapsed.
  - Source: [Commutation Act 1784 (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutation_Act_1784).

## Production rules for this one (user feedback on the previous short)
- **One consistent 3D world:** no flat 2D cut-away screens.
- **Particles look hand-made in a 3D tool:** tea leaves, steam, splashes, dust and spray are pre-rendered as lit, shaded 3D sprite sequences with motion blur. No flat circles, emoji stars or sticker labels.
- **Always something happening:** background acting, continuous camera moves, objects that pour, swing, click and splash.

## Hook plan (first ~3 s: maximum motion, no static setup)
- **Frame 1:** the camera is already pushing in fast as the lady snaps the caddy lid shut. The lid bounces, and a puff of 3D tea leaves spills out.
- **"only she had the key":** she spins the key with a click. A whip pan to a key close-up, then she drops it down her bodice with a smug look.
- **"if the servants got in there":** a quick pan to three servants peeking around the door, stacked like a totem pole, who duck away in sequence.
- **"half the tea would vanish":** a crash zoom on the caddy as a maid's hand sneaks a spoonful, and the tea level drains in a satisfying pour.
