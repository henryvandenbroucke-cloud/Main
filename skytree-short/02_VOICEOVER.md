# Step 2: Voiceover script and audio   (Opus 5.5, high; ElevenLabs eleven_v4)

START PROMPT:
"Read skytree-short/00_PLAN.md, STATUS.md and outputs/01_story/*. Do step 2 only: write the voiceover script and generate it with ElevenLabs."

Rules (from the study of 20 shorts and your own earlier scripts):
- 3.5-4.1 words per second, about 220-250 words for 60-65 s. About 13 words per sentence, contractions, plain English, no jargon without a one-line explanation.
- Fast and emotional, but use emotion tags sparingly (about one per 2-3 sentences, not every line). Never describe on screen what the viewer already sees.
- Voice starts at frame 1-4. No intro, no outro, no call to action. Ends on the last word of the payoff.
- Generate 2-3 takes, pick one, create words.json (word timings, any speech-to-text with word times) and hand-fix the timings.
- Spell out hard names for the voice (e.g. "Skytree", "shinbashira") or use a pronunciation dictionary.
Done when: SCRIPT.md (with word count and seconds), voice file name, words.json saved. Big audio files stay on the user's PC.
